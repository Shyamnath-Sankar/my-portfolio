THE BRIEF

The best model for the moment should be one tap away.

AI moves too quickly to live inside a single tab or provider. AnyLLM is a unified chat workspace that gives users the freedom to switch models, compare points of view, and bring their own keys without breaking flow.

THE BUILD

Model choice without the context switching.

Built in Flutter for a native cross-platform feel, AnyLLM brings a broad set of LLM APIs into one cohesive conversation surface. MCP support, web search, and a debate mode extend the app from chat tool to thinking environment.

  • Unified workspace for leading AI model providers
  • OpenAI-compatible BYOK API-provider support
  • Model debate mode to compare perspectives
  • Web search and MCP server integration

THE OUTCOME

100+

Published on Google Play and earning its first revenue in 30 days.

AnyLLM proved that a carefully scoped independent product can reach real users fast: it crossed 100 downloads and began generating revenue within its first month.
